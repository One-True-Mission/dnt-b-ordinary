Shipping & Returns

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Here is the honest version of how this works, because it is not the same as buying from a warehouse.

The policies that actually govern your order are the ones shown at checkout. Orders are placed on our Printify Pop-Up Store, and that store and its fulfillment partner handle production, shipping, delivery, and any resolution afterward. Read the shipping and return terms displayed at checkout before you buy, because those are the terms you are agreeing to.

Everything is made to order

Nothing here is printed in advance and sitting on a shelf. When you place an order, that item gets produced for you and then shipped. That is why the timeline is different from a retailer shipping something out of a bin, and it is also why standard return terms may not apply the same way they would to stock merchandise.

Who ships your order

The fulfillment partner behind the store produces and ships it. Dn't B' Ordinary does not hold inventory, does not pack boxes, and does not run the carrier relationship. Shipping options, costs, and estimates are shown to you during checkout.

Returns, replacements, and problems

If something arrives damaged, misprinted, or wrong, that is handled through the store where you checked out, under the policies shown there. Start with the order confirmation email you received, since it links back to the right place.

You can also send us a message. We cannot override the fulfillment partner's terms, but we can point you at the right process and follow up.

What this page does not do

This page does not promise a delivery date, a return window, a refund period, or a guarantee. Publishing terms we do not control would be worse than useless to you. The checkout page is the authority, and this page exists to say so clearly.

Questions before you buy

If you want to know how something ships before you order it, email hello@dntbordinary.com and ask.

This page is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice. It should be checked against the store's live terms and reviewed by an attorney if you want certainty.
