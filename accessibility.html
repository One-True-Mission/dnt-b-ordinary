Accessibility

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This site is built to be usable by as many people as possible, including people using screen readers, keyboard navigation, or magnification.

What we aim for

We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. That is the standard most organizations use, and it covers contrast, keyboard access, text alternatives, and clear structure.

What is already built in

  • Semantic HTML with one main landmark and a logical heading order on every page
  • Descriptive alternative text on content images
  • Keyboard access to every link, button, form field, and the image carousel
  • Visible focus states and labeled form fields
  • Color contrast checked against AA on body text and buttons
  • Support for the reduced motion setting, which stops the moving strip and the carousel
  • Layout that works from a phone screen up to a desktop monitor

Where it may fall short

This is a small site maintained by one person, and accessibility work is ongoing rather than finished. If a page, a design image, or a form gives you trouble, that is worth telling us about. It is the fastest way anything gets fixed.

Our store is a separate site

Checkout happens on our Printify Pop-Up Store, which we do not build or control. If you hit an accessibility barrier there, email us and we will help you place the order another way.

Tell us

Email hello@dntbordinary.com and describe what happened and what you were trying to do. We will get back to you and, where we can, give you the information another way in the meantime.
