The story

One guy, a notebook, and a printer

No design team. No warehouse. Just a person who kept writing things down until they turned into something you could wear.

Brian Rudd Founder
Who's behind it

It started with the sayings

Brian Rudd writes them. Not slogans, not taglines pulled off a list. The way people actually talk, on a good day and a bad one, written down before it got away.

The shirts came after. A saying does not do much sitting in a notebook, so each one gets built into a design and put on something people will actually use. Shirts, mugs, hats, metal signs, and posters big enough to read from across the room.

Dn't B' Ordinary opened in 2026 out of a house in the Buda, Texas area. It is a one-person operation and that is not a temporary condition. Every line, every design decision, and every reply to your email comes from the same guy.

How it runs

Three things worth knowing

The line comes first

Nothing gets designed until the saying earns it. If the words are not worth reading twice, the shirt is not worth printing.

Made to order

Your order is printed after you buy it, by the fulfillment partner. Nothing sits in a box waiting for a buyer, and nothing gets thrown out because it did not sell.

Written, not borrowed

Every saying on this site is original. None of it was licensed, scraped, or bought off a design marketplace.

Straight talk

When this is not the right call

If you need fifty matching shirts for a team by Friday, call a local screen printer. This is a one-at-a-time operation built around single orders, and a shop with a press in the back will beat it on a bulk job every time.

And a note on two of the collections. The Sobriety and Mental Health Awareness designs are exactly that: designs. They are not affiliated with any recovery program, treatment provider, or nonprofit, and they are not support, treatment, or advice. If you need help, please talk to someone qualified to give it. A shirt is a shirt.
